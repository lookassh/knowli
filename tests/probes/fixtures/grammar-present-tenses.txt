Unit 6 — Our weekend (fragment podręcznika A1, fikcyjny)

Words: play football, read a book, cook dinner, watch TV, jump, sing a song, listen to music, make a cake, write a letter, dance, ride a bike, swim in the lake, run in the park, sit on the sofa, stop at the corner, lie on the grass, tie your shoes.
Nouns: garden, kitchen, bike, lake, park, cake, letter, football, sofa, shoes, grass, song.

Dialogue: Look! Tom is riding his bike in the park. Mum is cooking dinner in the kitchen. The children are swimming in the lake.

Every day: I get up at seven. We play football on Mondays. My dad cooks dinner every day. Anna reads a book in the evening. They usually watch TV after school.
